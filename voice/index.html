Techne · Brand Concept and Voice

The Common Language

VOCABULARY, GRAMMAR, AND REGISTER FOR EVERYTHING TECHNE SAYS · JUNE 2026
STATUS: WORKING DRAFT · FOR MEMBER COMMENT ALONGSIDE THE GOVERNING DOCUMENTS

A cooperative whose thesis is that the maker and the made are one thing cannot speak carelessly, because its speech is also one of its products. This document holds the common language: the small vocabulary Techne keeps, the grammar its sentences follow, and the registers it moves between. It is written in the voice it describes, which is the only honest way to write a voice guide. Like the entity itself, it is a draft until the members say otherwise.

The Telos, in One Sentence

Integral technology: technology held as a living practice, in common, by the people who make it and the people who want it to exist. Every choice below serves that sentence. When a word or a construction does not serve it, the word goes, however familiar it feels. The brand is not a layer applied to the cooperative; it is the cooperative, audible.

Five Pillars of the Voice
PILLAR 1

Unhurried

The voice describes and waits. It never sells, never counts down, never manufactures scarcity. A door is open or it is not; urgency is the grammar of extraction, and Techne does not borrow the grammar of what it is built against. The aim is an invitation a person can decline without friction and return to without shame.

PILLAR 2

Plain about money and law

Candor is the brand's most distinctive asset because it is the rarest. Money sentences state what is given, what is kept, and what is not promised: your hundred dollars is gone the day you give it. Legal sentences name their own status: proposed, unratified, awaiting counsel. Where our precedent is candid, we are plainer still.

PILLAR 3

Material and etymological

Words are tools, and the voice treats them the way a craftsperson treats tools: knowing where they came from and what they were made to do. Technē is traced to the potter and the shipwright. The voice prefers words with hands: craft, practice, gathering, threshold, work. Abstractions earn their place or lose it.

PILLAR 4

Grounded in living systems, honestly

The ecological register is native, not decorative: seed, cross-pollination, keystone, membrane, habitat. It is used because information systems were built in reflection of living ones, through pattern recognition and biomimicry, and the voice says so rather than borrowing the metaphor silently. The place is real too: the voice knows it lives in Boulder, at the foot of real mountains, in real seasons.

PILLAR 5

Status-honest

Every artifact says what it is: verified, proposed, or an open question for counsel. The status chip is brand furniture, as characteristic as the typeface. Open questions are named as open in the headline, not the footnote, because a cooperative that learns must be able to say what it does not yet know.

The Grammar · How Sentences Behave

The unformed speaks in the conditional, honestly. Until ratification, Techne, LCA would be and intends to; it never is. The false present tense is the first lie a brand tells, and this one does not tell it. After ratification the documents switch tense in a single visible edit, which is itself a small ceremony.

Conditions, not dates. Sentences anchor to gates: when counsel signs, when the floor is reached, when the members ratify. Dates appear as aims and are always subordinated in the same sentence that states them.

Short declaratives for the things that matter most. One member, one vote. The money is gone the day you give it. The threshold is an assertion of trust. The voice earns its longer, unhurried sentences by spending its shortest ones on the load-bearing facts.

Attribution is conserved. Borrowed ideas arrive with their makers' names: Eno's scenius, Subvert's pattern, Engelbart's augmentation. A community of practice that absorbed its sources silently would be practicing extraction at the level of ideas.

House marks. No exclamation points outside quoted speech. No emoji anywhere; where iconography is needed, the stroke icon system carries it. Key terms take italics on first use. Lists are written into prose where prose can hold them. The pause is made with a comma, a colon, or a parenthesis, and sentences are built so the long interruption is not needed.

Registers · One Voice, Three Distances

The voice does not change character between rooms, but it changes distance. In the public register, facing supporters and the curious, the working word is cooperation: warm, concrete, plain about money, generous with etymology. In the working register, facing members and the board, the working word is coordination: the same candor with more machinery showing, gates and registers and counsel questions by name. In the legal register, the documents themselves, plain-language explainers stand beside the operative text, because the legal craft is itself technē and a member should be able to read what they are joining.

The quarantine is a brand rule, not only a tax rule. Techne's documents speak Subchapter T: patronage refunds, notices of allocation, income when received. RegenHub's speak Subchapter K: distributive shares, capital accounts, income when allocated. No sentence mixes them, no matter how convenient the mixture, because the cooperative's deepest editorial principle is that each thing says truly what it is. The grammar quarantine is where brand voice and legal integrity turn out to be the same discipline.

The Visual Voice, Briefly

The visual system already exists and this document defers to it: the co-op.us system, dark-first and adaptive, Libre Baskerville carrying the prose and IBM Plex Mono carrying the labels, terracotta spent sparingly so it keeps its warmth, stroke icons and never emoji, motion at four hundred milliseconds and stilled entirely for those who prefer reduced motion. Two notes belong here because they are voice as much as design. The status chip is part of the brand's face: verified, proposed, and counsel marks appear wherever claims appear, including in public. And the serif-for-prose, mono-for-machinery pairing is the visual form of the registers above: the warm voice and the honest instrument, always together, never confused.

Standing of This Document

A working draft, prepared by organizer research with AI assistance from the introduction, the blueprint, the pathway, and the working conversation on the guidebook. It binds no one. Like the bylaws it will sit beside, it is published for member comment, and the members who ratify the entity inherit the language too, to keep or to change. A common language belongs to the people who speak it, which means the last word here is deliberately not the last word.
