About Parnassus House

Parnassus House is an independent education organization founded by Paul Diduch and Niko Kovacevic. It offers seminars, tutoring, and workshops rooted in Socratic close reading. This series is hosted at RegenHub in Boulder — bringing structured intellectual inquiry into the studio.
