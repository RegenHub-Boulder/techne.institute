Techne
Brand Brief · May 2026 · For Octavian
Project
Brand identity system for Techne (techne.coop) — a new cooperative program and membership class of RegenHub, LCA — launching to support member recruitment across Boulder-Denver and aligned virtual communities.
Organization
Techne is a cooperative program of RegenHub, LCA — a Colorado Public Benefit Limited Cooperative Association — cultivating collective intelligence through right relationship between tools and the people who use them. Distinctive because it roots economic membership in a body of work, not just a fee.
Audience

The practitioner who builds things and thinks about how. Already embedded in some community of practice — a design studio, a civic tech project, a learning cohort. Has looked at both startup culture and cooperative culture and found each half-right.

Drawn to Boulder because place still matters, or connected to that scene virtually. Reads intentionally. Notices craft. Bristles at extraction. Would pay $25 for a relationship, not a subscription.

Not demographics — sensibilities. Illich on convivial tools. Alexander on pattern languages. Eno on scenius.

Positioning
Cooperative, not accelerator.
Members share in what they build together — not in what they pitch to investors.
Inquiry, not platform.
The question of right relationship with tools is held open. Techne provides conditions, not answers.
Rooted, not distributed.
Boulder-based and virtually extended. Place is a feature, not a limitation.
Call to Action
01 Explore — learn what Techne is and how it works
02 Join — $25 one-time member share
03 Connect — schedule an onboarding call with an existing member
Personality
Technical but humane.
Grounded but generative.
Precise but open-handed.

The Techne signature: lead with the word before asking people to live inside it. Etymology as method. Confidence without enclosure.

Constraints
Must-haves
  • Works in single color — stamp, letterhead, small format.
  • Holds at favicon and 16px icon scale.
  • Reads cooperative and craft-rooted, not startup or NGO.
  • Carries forward the techne.institute typographic register: serif authority + mono precision.
Must-avoids
  • Leaf, sprout, mycelium, network-node imagery — overused in the regen space.
  • Gradient, glow, or neon platform aesthetic.
  • "Impact," "ecosystem," "scale" as primary language.
  • Generic DAO or Web3 visual codes.
  • Accelerator orange, Helvetica, growth-mindset energy.
