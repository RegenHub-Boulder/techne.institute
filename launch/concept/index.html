The Concept

The cooperative has governing documents, a public benefit purpose, and an operating information system. What it lacks is a coherent way for new members to encounter all three in sequence — and to understand how they fit together before they sign anything.

The launch package is a co-op entry experience: a multi-step journey that orients incoming members to what RegenHub, LCA is, what membership means, what tools they'll use, and what they're agreeing to. It is the front door — shared across both the physical workspace and the virtual community of practice.

Techne is a program of RegenHub. Members join either through the workspace (physical presence at 1515 Walnut) or through the virtual community of practice (Techne.coop). The journey is the same through the first and third steps onward — it forks only at Step 2, where workspace context diverges from virtual CoP context.

Entry Point A
Workspace Membership
Physical coworking at 1515 Walnut. Stripe billing, access codes, monthly meetups, Ethereum Hub grant obligations. Augments the existing RegenHub.xyz member portal.
regenhub.xyz → /join
Entry Point B
Virtual Community of Practice
Participation in the Techne cooperative without a physical desk. Governance, patronage, ventures, and coordination — fully remote.
techne.coop → /join

Foundation
The Cooperative
Public Benefit Statement
What RegenHub, LCA is — a Colorado Limited Cooperative Association with a legally-filed public benefit purpose. Cultivating scenius: the collective intelligence that emerges from sustained proximity of culturally aligned people. Why a cooperative, not a company. What democratic control means in practice. The relationship between RegenHub (the space) and Techne (the program).

Context
Your Membership Context

Step 2 · Branching
Workspace Branch
The Space
The physical hub at 1515 Walnut, Boulder. The Ethereum Everywhere grant — what it funds, what it requires, what that means for how the space operates. Co-working access, monthly Ethereum meetups, the CROPS design intentions. What workspace membership includes operationally.
Source: Hub Operations PRD
Virtual CoP Branch
The Community
Techne as program of RegenHub. What the virtual community of practice is — how it differs from the workspace, what participation means without a desk. The relationship to governance, patronage, and the tools that enable distributed cooperation.
Source: CIS PRD · Techne Program Model
3
Infrastructure
The Common Information System
CIS PRD
The cooperative's information infrastructure — what it is, why it was built rather than bought. An overview of the modules members will have access to: people, agreements, treasury, roadmap, activity, patronage. The patronage model: how labor, revenue, cash, and community contributions translate into capital accounts and surplus allocation.

All Members
4
Governance
How Decisions Are Made
Bylaws v.2
Democratic member control — one member, one vote. The four membership classes and what each holds. How the board relates to the member body. What members decide versus what is delegated to stewards. The graduated decision threshold structure: day-to-day → board resolutions → bylaws amendments → public benefit. Link to the full bylaws v.2 for depth.

All Members
5
Agreement
The Member Agreement
Member Agreement v.2
Plain-language orientation to the member agreement — not legalese, but the substance. IP scope: what members bring stays theirs, what the cooperative builds together belongs to the cooperative. Withdrawal mechanics. Patronage accounting and what it means for capital accounts. Three blocking issues currently in organizer review. Alignment signal at end of step. Full document linked throughout.

All Members
6
Profile
Your Identity
CIS · auth.user_metadata
Name, role, location, bio. Membership type selection — workspace or virtual CoP — which determines the CIS modules and communications you receive. Persisted to Supabase user_metadata on completion. This is the existing /enroll identity step, promoted to its proper place in the sequence.

All Members
7
Completion
Welcome
CIS · /intranet
Confirmation of enrollment. Next steps specific to membership type — workspace members see access code instructions and billing setup; virtual CoP members see governance participation and patronage orientation. Links into the full CIS. An acknowledgment that the cooperative is in formation and the member is joining a living process, not a finished institution.
