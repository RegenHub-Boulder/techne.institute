CIS Substrate & Modules
Build roadmap · v0.2 · 2026-05-22

The Common Information System is the cooperative's book of record — member registry, capital accounts, agreements, and governance events. It is built in two stages: the floor (substrate infrastructure) followed by five CIS modules. Phases 0, 1, and 3 are complete as of May 22. Phase 2 (bylaws ingestion) is the active next step.
