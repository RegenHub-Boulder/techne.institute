B · Capital · 14

Labor-Hour Mutual Credit & Labor-Patronage Cash-Out

Dual-ledger system. One attested labor event projects to two places: a closed-loop mutual-credit ledger denominated in labor-hours, and a patronage measure that drives § 704 allocations and § 731 cash distributions.

IRC § 704 · § 731 · § 6045 · C.R.S. 7-58-102 · REA / ValueFlows · EAS attestation · PRD: Module 14 PRD ↗
Status

Phased rollout

Phases 0–4
Phase 0Foundations Identity and event-log spine. EAS labor-event schema registered, attestations on testnet. KPI: deterministic balance replay.
Phase 1Mutual-credit pilot Cohort transacts with enforced credit limits and closed-loop redemption. KPI: active accounts, clearing volume, zero fiat-redemption leakage.
Phase 2Patronage & books Capital accounts computed from event log; reconciled to Xero. 1099-B and K-1 data producible. KPI: near-zero reconciliation variance.
Phase 3Periodic cash-out Patronage Plan ratified, weighting set, tax elections made, counsel sign-off. Board-ratified allocation → 731 distribution via Mercury. blocked · open deps
Phase 4Exit redemption Departing member's capital account returned per C.R.S. 7-58-1007 solvency limits. KPI: exits completed within policy.
Architecture · Dual Ledger

One event log, two projections

REA / ValueFlows · EAS attested

A single EAS-countersigned labor event feeds the append-only log. From it, two projections are computed — never stored. Balances are replayed from events.

Ledger One · Mutual Credit
  • Denominated in labor-hours
  • Giver's balance rises, receiver's falls
  • All balances net to zero
  • Closed-loop: spend on member goods only
  • Not redeemable for fiat
Ledger Two · Patronage Measure
  • Same event accrues as patronage
  • Drives the § 704 allocation
  • Builds the member's capital account
  • Leads to the § 731 cash distribution
  • Board ratified at period close

Compliance Perimeters
Not money transmission

Co-op pays its own members their share of its own income. Does not accept and forward third-party value (31 CFR 1010.100(ff)).

Not a payment stablecoin

No fixed-value redemption obligation and no stable-value representation — outside the GENIUS Act definition.

Not a security

Units are non-transferable membership rights tied to use and patronage — not tradable instruments bought for passive return (Howey, Reves).

All three characterizations are provisional and fact-specific. Confirm with qualified cooperative, securities, BSA/fintech, and partnership-tax counsel before Phase 3.
