Techne Institute

This site is between drafts.

The work published here described an institute as it was imagined before it had been practiced. What comes next is emerging somewhere else first — in a launch party, in the Philosoraptor dialogues, in the ordinary argument of people building a cooperative in the open. When it has a shape worth publishing, it will be published here.

The earlier pages have not been deleted. They remain in the repository as an archive of what was thought at the time, and they are no longer served as a description of what is.

Techne Institute is a project of RegenHub, LCA, a Colorado limited cooperative association trading as techne.coop, where the cooperative's live record is kept.
